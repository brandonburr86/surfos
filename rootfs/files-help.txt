SurfOS Files: type files [directory] to browse the filesystem.
Arrows select; Enter opens a folder or edits a file; Left goes to the parent.
Home/End and Page Up/Down move around; / goes to root; r refreshes.
. shows/hides dotfiles; q returns to the shell.
In edit: Ctrl+S saves, Ctrl+Q returns to Files.
Mouse in SGR serial/Telnet terminals: select then click again to open,
or select and click Open/Edit. The native SurfOS screen uses the keyboard.
Devices and obvious binary files are refused; read-only mounts allow viewing.
