clear - clear the screen

SYNOPSIS
    clear

DESCRIPTION
    Writes the escape sequences that reset the colours, show the cursor,
    clear the screen and put the cursor at the top left, on this
    terminal, not on the console that happens to show. clear > file puts
    them in the file. Scrollback keeps what was on the screen, on a
    framebuffer console.

SEE ALSO
    ansi
