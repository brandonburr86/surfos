wcalc - the desktop's Calculator as a program

SYNOPSIS
    wcalc

DESCRIPTION
    A window on the desktop with whole numbers, + - * and /, within plus
    or minus 999,999,999. Click its buttons or type: digits, the
    operators, Enter or = calculates, Backspace takes a digit back, C
    clears. Esc, Ctrl+W or the close button end it. It needs a desktop:
    start it from the desktop's Terminal or from Apps > Run program.
