wmines - Mines, a game in a window

SYNOPSIS
    wmines

DESCRIPTION
    A field of covered cells, some of them mines. Reveal every cell
    that holds none: a number says how many mines its eight neighbours
    hold, and a cell with none around it opens its neighbours too. The
    first cell revealed never holds a mine, nor do its neighbours when
    the field has room. Flag the cells you take for mines; a click on a
    number whose flags are all placed opens the rest around it (a
    chord). The toolbar counts the mines left (less the flags) and the
    time, which runs from the first cell revealed.

    The fields: Small, 10 by 8 with 10 mines; Medium, 16 by 12 with 32;
    Large, 28 by 15 with 80. The window opens large enough for the
    large field, and the cells grow or shrink with it.

    It needs a desktop: Apps > Programs > Mines or its tile starts it,
    or wmines & in the desktop's Terminal. Its Game menu is in the
    desktop's bar while its window has the focus. The best time of each
    field and the games won last while the window is open; nothing is
    written anywhere.

KEYS
    Click           reveal a cell; on a number, chord
    Right-click     flag a cell, or take the flag away
    Middle button   chord
    Arrows, Home,   move the cursor (the first key shows it), to the
    End, Page keys  row's or the column's end
    Space, Enter    reveal the cell under the cursor, or chord
    F               flag it
    N, Ctrl+N       a new field of the same size
    1, 2, 3         a new small, medium or large field
    Esc, Ctrl+W     close the window, asking first during a game

    A key held down repeats only the arrows.
