ansi - the terminal's two sides, by hand

SYNOPSIS
    ansi
    rawkeys

DESCRIPTION
    ansi draws a test pattern with the escape sequences the console
    knows, laid out for the screen tests, and says the terminal's size
    at the end. rawkeys puts the terminal in raw mode and shows the
    bytes each key sends, in hexadecimal, until q; it reads the keyboard
    as it goes, so its output cannot go to a file.

EXAMPLES
    ansi
    rawkeys
