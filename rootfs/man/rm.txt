rm - delete files and directories

SYNOPSIS
    rm [-rf] path...

DESCRIPTION
    Deletes files. There is no way back. kernel rm deletes files, with
    no options.

OPTIONS
    -r, -R  Directories too, with everything in them.
    -f      Say nothing about paths that do not exist.

EXAMPLES
    rm /hda1/OLD.TXT
    rm -r /hda1/site

SEE ALSO
    rmdir, mv
