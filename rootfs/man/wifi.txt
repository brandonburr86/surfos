wifi - Wi-Fi

SYNOPSIS
    wifi up | down | scan | status | leave
    wifi join ssid [bssid]
    wifi save [ssid]
    wifi forget ssid
    wifi saved | connect
    wifiinfo
    wlansim on|off ssid

DESCRIPTION
    Drives the first Wi-Fi device, wlan0. up starts the card and down
    stops it. scan lists what is in range, strongest first, with its
    security in words. join joins a network, open or WPA2, scanning
    first when it is not in the list; an SSID may have spaces. A WPA2
    password is asked for with no echo, turned into a key at once, and
    never shown, kept or logged. status gives the state, the last
    failure and the address, and leave leaves the network. An address
    comes from dhcp wlan0.

    Saved networks are kept on the stick SurfOS was started from, in
    surfos-config/networks.conf, with their keys and never a password.
    wifi save saves the network joined now, or asks for another's
    password; forget removes one; saved lists them; connect does what a
    start does. While telnetd or ftpd runs, SurfOS joins no open network
    by itself.

    wifiinfo prints what a Wi-Fi driver needs to know about this
    machine's card, reading only. wlansim switches the simulated access
    points that the kernel word wlansim makes, for tests.

    Only the laptop's Intel 9560 has a driver. QEMU has no card without
    wlansim.

EXAMPLES
    wifi up
    wifi scan
    wifi join "My Network"
    wifi save
