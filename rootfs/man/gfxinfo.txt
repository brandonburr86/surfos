gfxinfo - the framebuffer, and a graphics test

SYNOPSIS
    gfxinfo
    gfxtest [seconds]

DESCRIPTION
    gfxinfo says what the framebuffer is, what the console has cost it
    in presents and cells, and each desktop's frame times. gfxtest shows
    a test pattern of colours, lines and a picture for 1 to 60 seconds,
    5 unless given; Ctrl+C or another console goes back to text.

    There is a framebuffer only on the UEFI USB image: not in VGA text
    mode, so not under plain QEMU.
