jobs - the jobs of this shell

SYNOPSIS
    jobs
    fg [%n]
    bg [%n]
    wait [%n|pid...]

DESCRIPTION
    A job is what one command started. cmd & starts one in the
    background, and Ctrl+Z stops the one in the foreground. jobs lists
    them: number, state, command. fg brings job n, or the last, to the
    foreground again; bg lets it go on in the background. wait waits for
    those to end, or all of them; Ctrl+C ends the wait.

    %n is a job's number, %% and %+ the last job, %- the one before; a
    job's pid works too. A job in the background that reads the terminal
    stops until fg gives it the keys. When the shell ends, its stopped
    jobs are killed.

    A surfsh that run started in the ring-0 shell has no job control: fg
    and bg say so.

EXAMPLES
    count 100 100 &
    jobs
    fg %1
