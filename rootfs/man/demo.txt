demo - the 2004 demonstrations

SYNOPSIS
    demo
    hanoi
    beep
    funky

DESCRIPTION
    demo is the 2004 menu: exception handling, string comparison and a
    colourful console, with q to leave. hanoi prints the moves of the
    Towers of Hanoi for a number of discs, typed as one digit, until 0.
    beep sounds the PC speaker at 2000 Hz for a second. funky plays a
    tune in a task of its own and returns at once.

    demo and hanoi read the keyboard as they go, so their output cannot
    go to a file.
