acpi - the firmware's ACPI tables

SYNOPSIS
    acpi
    acpidump DSDT | SSDT [instance]
    acpinvs hex-address bytes

DESCRIPTION
    acpi lists the tables the firmware gave: each one's signature,
    address and length, whether it passed its checks, and the instance
    number of each SSDT. They are read only with profile=laptop;
    elsewhere acpi says so.

    acpidump prints the bytes of a checked DSDT or SSDT in hexadecimal,
    for study on another computer. acpinvs prints 1 to 256 bytes of ACPI
    NVS memory, and refuses a range that is not ACPI NVS below 4 GB.
    Nothing here runs the tables' code; all three only read.

EXAMPLES
    acpi
    acpidump SSDT 2 > /usb1p1/SSDT2.TXT
