base64 - encode and decode base64

SYNOPSIS
    base64 [-d] [-w columns] [file]

DESCRIPTION
    Writes a file, or standard input, as base64 text in lines of 76
    columns. With -d it goes the other way: line ends are passed over,
    and anything else outside the alphabet is refused with status 2,
    after what came before it. Both stream, so a file may be any size.

OPTIONS
    -d           Decode.
    -w columns   The width of the lines. 0 makes one line, with no
                 newline at its end.

    --decode and --wrap=N are the same.

EXAMPLES
    base64 /hda1/LOGO.BMP > /hda1/LOGO.B64
    base64 -d /hda1/LOGO.B64 > /hda1/LOGO.BMP
