df - size and free space of the mounted volumes

SYNOPSIS
    df [path...]

DESCRIPTION
    Shows, for / and every mount point under it, or for the volumes that
    hold the paths given: Device, Type, Size, Used, Avail, Use%, Mounted
    on and Label. Sizes are in powers of 1024, as 16 MB or 1.9 GB. A
    volume mounted read-only says so after its label, and one that does
    not count its space shows -.

    The status is 1 when a volume could not say its size. kernel df is
    the kernel's command, with the same columns, for the volumes on
    devices.

EXAMPLES
    df
    df /hda1

SEE ALSO
    du, lsblk, mount
