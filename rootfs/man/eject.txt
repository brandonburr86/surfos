eject - make a disk safe to pull out

SYNOPSIS
    eject disk

DESCRIPTION
    Writes out and unmounts all of a disk: disk is usb0, or one of its
    partitions. Every mount is written out first, and none goes when one
    cannot be, so a disk that refuses writes stays mounted, and eject
    says so. Only when the disk has confirmed its own cache does eject
    say safe to remove.

    Ctrl+C before the unmounting starts leaves the disk mounted; once it
    has started, it finishes. A disk already pulled out is only let go
    of.

EXAMPLES
    eject usb1

SEE ALSO
    mount, sync, lsblk
