gui - the desktop

SYNOPSIS
    gui

DESCRIPTION
    Opens the SurfOS desktop on this console: windows, menus, Notepad,
    Files, a Terminal and the other applications. Ctrl+Q goes back to
    the shell, Ctrl+E opens Notepad and Ctrl+B Files. The Help menu has
    the rest.

    It needs the active local console on the UEFI framebuffer: not over
    telnet, not in VGA text mode, and not on a console that does not
    show. It reads the keyboard as it goes, so its output cannot go to a
    file.
