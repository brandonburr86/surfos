export - share variables, or fix them

SYNOPSIS
    export [name[=value]...]
    readonly [name[=value]...]

DESCRIPTION
    export gives the variables to the programs started from here, in
    their environment. readonly keeps them as they are for the rest of
    this shell. Alone, or with -p, each lists its variables.

EXAMPLES
    export PATH=/initrd/bin:/hda1/bin
    readonly LIMIT=10

SEE ALSO
