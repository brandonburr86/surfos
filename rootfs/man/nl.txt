nl - number the lines of files

SYNOPSIS
    nl [-b style] [-w width] [-s sep] [-v first] [-i step] [file...]

DESCRIPTION
    Writes the lines with numbers before them, counting on from file
    to file. Lines not numbered are indented as far as the numbers.
    A last line without a newline gets one.

    Other systems' page sections, lines of \: and the like, are not
    known here: they are lines like any other.

OPTIONS
    -b style   Which lines get a number: t those with text (the
               default), a all, n none, pBRE those a basic regular
               expression matches. pBRE needs libsurf's regular
               expressions; a build without them says so.
    -w width   The number's width: 6.
    -s sep     What comes after the number: a TAB.
    -v first   The first number: 1.
    -i step    How much each goes up: 1.

EXAMPLES
    nl -b a /hda1/NOTES.TXT
    nl -w 3 -s ': ' list.txt
