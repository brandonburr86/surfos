echo - print words

SYNOPSIS
    echo [-n] [-e] [word...]

DESCRIPTION
    Prints the words with single spaces between them and a newline. In
    surfsh this is its own command; the program /initrd/bin/echo does
    the same, for where there is no shell to ask, and kernel echo prints
    its words with no options.

OPTIONS
    -n  No newline at the end.
    -e  Turn \n \r \t and \\ into the characters they name.

EXAMPLES
    echo hello > /hda1/HELLO.TXT
    echo -n "no line end"
