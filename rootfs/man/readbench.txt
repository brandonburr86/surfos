readbench - read speed of a block device

SYNOPSIS
    readbench device [MB]

DESCRIPTION
    Reads MB megabytes from the start of the device, 8 unless given, in
    requests of 64 KB, through the block layer, and says the speed. It
    writes nothing. Ctrl+C stops it.

EXAMPLES
    readbench usb0 32

SEE ALSO
    fsbench, lsblk, hexdump
