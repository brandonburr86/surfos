wc - count lines, words and bytes

SYNOPSIS
    wc [-l] [-w] [-c] [file...]

DESCRIPTION
    Prints the lines, words and bytes of each file, or of standard input
    without files, and a total line for more than one file. It counts
    bytes, not characters.

OPTIONS
    -l  Lines.
    -w  Words.
    -c  Bytes. With none of the three, all of them.

EXAMPLES
    ls /initrd/bin | wc -l
