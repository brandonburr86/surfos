sleep - wait for a while

SYNOPSIS
    sleep [-u] [ms]

DESCRIPTION
    Waits for ms MILLISECONDS, not seconds, 1000 unless given, and says
    how long that was. sleep 5 is five thousandths of a second. A kill
    or Ctrl+C cuts it short.

OPTIONS
    -u  Sleep as a driver's delay does, where nothing cuts it short: a
        test of the kill that waits.

EXAMPLES
    sleep 1500
    sleep 200; echo done
