comm - compare two sorted files line by line

SYNOPSIS
    comm [-123] file1 file2

DESCRIPTION
    Writes three columns: lines only in file1, lines only in file2,
    and lines in both, each column a TAB further in. Lines compare by
    their bytes, as sort without options sorts them. The order is not
    checked: input out of order gives what a merge of it gives. - is
    standard input, for one of the files.

OPTIONS
    -1    Leave out the first column.
    -2    Leave out the second.
    -3    Leave out the third.

EXAMPLES
    comm -12 old.txt new.txt

SEE ALSO
    sort, diff, join, uniq
