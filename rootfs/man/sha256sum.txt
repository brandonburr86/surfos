sha256sum - SHA-256 of files

SYNOPSIS
    sha256sum [file...]
    sha256sum -c [file...]

DESCRIPTION
    Prints the SHA-256 of each file, or of standard input without one or
    for -, as lines of the hash, two spaces and the name.

OPTIONS
    -c  Read such lines from the files, or from standard input, and
        check each file named: name: OK, or name: FAILED and status 1. A
        file that cannot be read says why, and lines that are neither
        are counted and said at the end.

EXAMPLES
    sha256sum /initrd/bin/* > /hda1/SUMS.TXT
    sha256sum -c /hda1/SUMS.TXT
