paste - put the lines of files side by side

SYNOPSIS
    paste [-d list] [-s] [file...]

DESCRIPTION
    Writes the first lines of all the files on one line, then the
    second lines, separated by a TAB. - is standard input, and may be
    named several times to take a line from it for each.

    A program has 16 descriptors, so side by side about a dozen files
    can be open. With -s any number will do.

OPTIONS
    -d list   Take the separators from list in turn, starting again
              on each line. An entry is one character, or \n, \t, \\
              or \0, which means nothing.
    -s        Put all of each file's lines on one line, a file at a
              time.

EXAMPLES
    paste names.txt ages.txt
    seq 6 | paste - - -
    paste -s -d , list.txt
