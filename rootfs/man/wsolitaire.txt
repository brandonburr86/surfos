wsolitaire - Solitaire (Klondike), a card game in a window

SYNOPSIS
    wsolitaire

DESCRIPTION
    Klondike: seven piles dealt one to seven cards, the top one up; the
    rest is the stock. Build the four foundations up from the aces by
    suit, and the piles down in alternate colours; only a king, or a
    run from a king, goes on an empty pile. A face-up run moves
    together, and the card it uncovers turns up by itself. The stock
    deals one card or three to the waste, and takes the waste back as
    often as you like. A card may come back down from a foundation to
    the piles, but not to another foundation.

    Drag a card (and the cards on it) to a pile, or click it to send it
    where it can go: a single card to its foundation first, then to the
    next pile it fits. Every move can be undone. Once no card on the
    piles is face down and the cards can all go home from there, the
    game finishes by itself. The toolbar counts the moves and the time.

    It needs a desktop: Apps > Programs > Solitaire or its tile starts
    it, or wsolitaire & in the desktop's Terminal. Its Game menu is in
    the desktop's bar while its window has the focus. While a game is
    under way, a new deal (by a key, the toolbar, drawing one or three,
    or the menu) and closing ask first, Cancel first. A game counts as
    played from its first move. The games won, the best time and the
    fewest moves, drawing one and three apart, last while the window is
    open; nothing is written anywhere.

KEYS
    Drag, click     move cards; a click on the stock deals
    Arrows          choose a card (Up goes deeper into a run)
    Enter           send the card chosen where it goes
    Space, D        deal from the stock
    U, Backspace,   undo
    Ctrl+Z
    N, Ctrl+N       deal again
    1, 3            deal anew, drawing one card or three
    Esc, Ctrl+W     close the window

    A key held down repeats only the arrows. A key that changes the
    game while a card is dragged lets go of it: the card goes back.

LIMITS
    Klondike only, drawing one or three; no other solitaire, no points
    (no Vegas or standard scoring), no limit on the passes through the
    stock. No hints, no pause (the clock runs on in the background), no
    sound. Undo goes back up to the last 1024 moves. Cards go home by
    themselves only at the end, once every card is up and the rest can
    all go home; drawing three, that is not always offered. The record
    is the session's: it is gone when the window closes.
