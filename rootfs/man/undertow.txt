undertow - a text web browser

SYNOPSIS
    undertow [address]
    undertow --dump address
    undertow --help

DESCRIPTION
    Reads pages on a terminal, one screen at a time, with a prompt under
    them. It shows text and numbered links: no pictures, no JavaScript.
    An address typed alone at the prompt opens too. Ctrl+C or Ctrl+D at
    the prompt quits.

    HTTPS checks the certificate chain, the name and the dates, with no
    way to skip any of it; it needs a CPU with RDRAND and a clock that
    is right. /initrd/etc/undertow.conf has its settings.

    --dump prints the page's text and then its links on standard output,
    and needs no terminal. The status is 0 for a 2xx response, 1 for any
    other or a failure, and 2 for a mistake in the words.

COMMANDS
    open address   Open an address. o too.
    NUMBER         Follow a link; go NUMBER too.
    back, forward  Through the history; b and f.
    reload         r.
    Enter, next    The next screen.
    prev, top      The one before, and the first.
    links          List the links.
    find words     Find text; findnext goes on.
    history        The pages seen.
    url            This page's address.
    clock          See or set the time HTTPS checks against.
    help           The commands; ? too.
    quit           End; q too.

EXAMPLES
    undertow https://example.com/
    undertow --dump example.com | head

SEE ALSO
