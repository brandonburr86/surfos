cmp - compare two files byte by byte

SYNOPSIS
    cmp [-l] [-s] [-n count] file1 file2

DESCRIPTION
    Says where two files first differ: the byte and the line, both
    from 1. Nothing is said when they are the same. If one ends first
    that goes to the standard error. Status 0 the same, 1 different,
    2 for trouble. - is standard input.

OPTIONS
    -l         List every byte that differs: its number in decimal,
               and the two values in octal.
    -s         Say nothing: only the status.
    -n count   Compare at most count bytes.

EXAMPLES
    cmp /hda1/A.BIN /hda1/B.BIN
    cmp -s a b && echo same
