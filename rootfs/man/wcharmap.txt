wcharmap - the desktop's Character Map as a program

SYNOPSIS
    wcharmap

DESCRIPTION
    A window with the console's 256 characters in a grid of 16 by 16,
    and the chosen one large with its code, its Unicode code point and
    its UTF-8 bytes. Enter, Ctrl+C, a double-click or the button copy it
    to the desktop's clipboard. Ctrl+L goes to a code. Esc, Ctrl+W or
    the close button end it. The desktop's Apps menu starts it, or
    brings its window back.

SEE ALSO
    gui, wclock, wcalc
