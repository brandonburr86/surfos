dmesg - the kernel log

SYNOPSIS
    dmesg

DESCRIPTION
    Prints the kernel's log, its last 16 KB. Lines that start with +
    come from drivers and services: +USB:, +PART:, +FAT, +NET:, +IWL:,
    +MDNS:. It takes no options; use grep.

EXAMPLES
    dmesg | grep -i usb
    dmesg > /usb1p1/DMESG.TXT
