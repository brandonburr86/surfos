history - the lines typed before

SYNOPSIS
    history [-w]

DESCRIPTION
    Lists the lines typed at the prompt, oldest first. The last 1,000
    are kept, and Up and Down recall them; a line that starts with a
    space is not. !! is the line before, !$ its last word, !12 line 12,
    !-2 the one before the last, and !ls the last line that started with
    ls.

    With the setting history on, surfsh keeps them in
    surfos-config/history on the stick SurfOS was started from, as plain
    text, and loads them at its start. Off, they go with the shell.

OPTIONS
    -w  Save them on the stick now.

EXAMPLES
    history | grep mount
