selftest - the kernel's self tests

SYNOPSIS
    selftest
    heaptest

DESCRIPTION
    selftest runs the kernel's tests, a line each, and ends with
    SELFTEST PASS, or STOPPED when Ctrl+C or a kill stopped it after the
    test under way. heaptest makes random allocations with patterns and
    then verifies the heap. One runs at a time, and a second is refused
    with status 1. selftest uses files on /hda1 and the scratch ramdisk
    rd1 when they are there.
