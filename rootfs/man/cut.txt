cut - pick parts of each line

SYNOPSIS
    cut -b list [file...]
    cut -c list [file...]
    cut -f list [-d delim] [-s] [file...]

DESCRIPTION
    Prints parts of each line of the files, or of standard input without
    one.

    A list is numbers from 1 and ranges, separated by commas: 3, 2-4, 5-
    (to the end), -3 (from the start). The parts come out in the line's
    order, each once, whatever order the list has them in.

    With -f, the chosen fields are joined by the delimiter again, and a
    line with no delimiter comes out whole.

OPTIONS
    -b list   Count bytes.
    -c list   Count characters: a UTF-8 sequence is one.
    -f list   Count fields.
    -d delim  The delimiter of -f, one character; a TAB without it.
    -s        With -f, leave out lines that have no delimiter.

EXAMPLES
    echo a:b:c | cut -d: -f2,3
    ls -l /hda1 | cut -c1-20
