less - a file or a pipeline's text a screenful at a time

SYNOPSIS
    less [file]

DESCRIPTION
    Shows a file, or standard input, on the alternate screen, and gives
    the shell's screen back when it ends. The bottom row says where you
    are. The text is read whole first, 8 MB at most: the rest is left
    unread, and the bottom row says so. The keys come from the terminal,
    so less can end a pipeline.

    Long lines are cut, with a > in the last column; Right and Left move
    sideways by half a screen. A TAB moves to the next multiple of 8,
    UTF-8 shows as characters, one cell each, control characters as ^X
    in inverse video, and a CR before a line's end is dropped.

    With standard output anything but a terminal, less copies the text
    there, as cat does.

KEYS
    Space f PgDn ^F
                    The next screenful. ^V too.
    b PgUp ^B       The one before.
    Enter j e Down  One line on. ^N and ^E too.
    k y Up          One line back. ^P and ^Y too.
    d ^D, u ^U      Half a screen on, and back.
    g < Home        The start.
    G > End         The end.
    / ?             Search forward from the top line, or back from the
                    line above it, for text, not a pattern. Case counts
                    only when the text has capitals. Enter alone
                    searches for the last text again. The line found
                    goes to the top, and matches show in inverse video.
    n, N            The next match, and the one the other way.
    Right, Left     Sideways.
    ^L              Draw the screen again.
    h               The keys.
    q Q ^C          End.

EXAMPLES
    dmesg | less
    less /hda1/NOTES.TXT
