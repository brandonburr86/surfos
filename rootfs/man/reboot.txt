reboot - restart or turn off the machine

SYNOPSIS
    reboot [-f]
    poweroff [-n]

DESCRIPTION
    reboot ends the programs, writes out and unmounts the read-write
    volumes, and resets the machine, each step a shutdown: line on F1.
    poweroff does the same and turns the machine off through ACPI.
    Ctrl+Alt+Del starts the same; pressed again, it resets at once.

OPTIONS
    -f  reboot: reset at once, writing nothing out.
    -n  poweroff: say what it would do, and do nothing.

SEE ALSO
    sync, eject
