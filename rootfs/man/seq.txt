seq - a sequence of whole numbers

SYNOPSIS
    seq [first [step]] last

DESCRIPTION
    Prints the whole numbers from first (1) to last by step (1), a line
    each. A step below 0 counts down. There are no decimals and no
    options.

EXAMPLES
    seq 5
    seq 10 -3 1
    for i in $(seq 3); do echo $i; done
