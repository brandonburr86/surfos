man - the manual

SYNOPSIS
    man name
    man -k word

DESCRIPTION
    Shows the page of a program, a kernel command or one of surfsh's own
    commands: through less on a terminal, plain when standard output is
    anything else. Pages are /initrd/man/NAME.txt. Like commands share a
    page, so man crashdiv shows crash; a page of a name's own comes
    first.

    The status is 0, 1 when there is no such page or no page has the
    word, and 2 for a mistake.

OPTIONS
    -k word  Every page whose first line has the word, without regard to
             case in ASCII letters, one line each, in order of name. The
             word is plain text, not a pattern.

EXAMPLES
    man ls
    man -k disk
    man sed | grep -n 'address'
