wswell - Swell, a falling-blocks game of SurfOS's own, in a window

SYNOPSIS
    wswell

DESCRIPTION
    Pieces fall into a sea 12 cells wide and 18 deep, a row at a time.
    Move and turn each as it falls; a row filled across is washed away,
    and the rows above come down. The pieces are SurfOS's own seven, of
    two, three and five cells: the pebble, the drift, the shell, the
    crest, the reef, the gull and the kelp. The outline at the bottom
    shows where the falling piece would come to rest. A piece that has
    landed rests a moment before it sets, so it can still be slid.

    Every 8 rows the level rises and the pieces fall faster. From level
    3 on, every 16 pieces the swell lifts the whole sea a row and leaves
    a row of sand with two gaps under it. Rows washed away at once score
    the level times 10, 25 or 50, and a sea left empty 200 times the
    level more; Down scores a point a row, Space two. The game ends when
    a piece has no room to come in, or the sea comes over the top.

    It needs a desktop: Apps > Programs > Swell or its tile starts it,
    or wswell & in the desktop's Terminal. The game pauses when its
    window loses the focus. Its Game menu is in the desktop's bar while
    its window has the focus. The best score lasts while the window is
    open; nothing is written anywhere.

KEYS
    Enter, Space    start a game; go on from a pause
    Left, Right     move the piece
    Up, X           turn it clockwise
    Z               turn it the other way
    Down            move it a row down; set it once it has landed
    Space           drop it
    P               pause, and go on
    N               a new game, once a game is paused or over
    Ctrl+N          a new game at once (Game > New game)
    Esc, Ctrl+W     close the window, asking first during a game

    Keys come as they are typed: hold Left, Right or Down and the
    keyboard's repeat moves the piece on. No other key repeats.
