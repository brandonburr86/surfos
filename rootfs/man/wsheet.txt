wsheet - the Spreadsheet: numbers, text and formulas in a grid

SYNOPSIS
    wsheet [file]

DESCRIPTION
    A window on the desktop with a sheet of 256 columns (A to IV) and
    65,536 rows. A cell holds a number, text, TRUE or FALSE, an error,
    or a formula: what is typed after =. Formulas are worked out again
    whenever anything changes, each after the cells it uses; a formula
    in a cycle shows #CYCLE!. Apps, its tile, and Files with a .sheet
    or a .csv start it; with a file named, it reads that file.

    A formula has numbers, "text", TRUE, FALSE, references (A1, $A$1,
    A$1, $A1) and ranges (A1:B5), the operators + - * / ^ % & and
    = <> < <= > >=, and the functions SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT,
    COUNTA, IF, AND, OR, NOT, ROUND, INT, ABS, SQRT, POWER, MOD, LEN,
    LEFT, RIGHT, MID, UPPER, LOWER, TRIM and CONCAT. -2^2 is 4 and
    2^3^2 is 64, as in other spreadsheets. A number shows its 15
    significant digits: =0.1+0.2 is 0.3. A formula that does not parse
    is kept as typed and shows #ERROR!; the status bar says why.

    A copied formula's references move with it ($ keeps a part where
    it is), and inserting or deleting rows and columns keeps every
    reference on the cell it meant; one to a cell that went is #REF!.
    The formats are General, fixed decimals and percent. Undo and Redo
    keep the last 100 changes.

    Its menus, File, Edit, Sheet and Format, are in the desktop's bar
    while its window has the focus, and their shortcuts work in it in
    any case. Open and Save as are the desktop's choosers.

KEYS
    typing           a cell's new content (Esc leaves it as it was)
    Enter, Tab       put it in and move down, right (Shift: back)
    arrows           move; with Shift select, with Ctrl jump to edges
    Ctrl+E           edit the cell in the formula bar (a double-click)
    Ctrl+L           go to a cell or a range, typed in its name
    Delete           empty the selected cells
    Ctrl+C, X, V     copy, cut, paste (text from elsewhere too)
    Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y   undo, redo
    Ctrl++, Ctrl+-   insert, delete rows
    Ctrl+S           save (Ctrl+Shift+S: save as)
    Ctrl+O, Ctrl+N   open a file, a new sheet
    Ctrl+W           close, asking first about unsaved changes

FILES
    A .sheet is the Spreadsheet's own text, a line a record, which
    keeps everything: formulas, formats and column widths. A .csv is
    RFC 4180's comma-separated values, opened as values (a field that
    begins with = is text), and saved as values: saving one asks first
    when formulas would be lost.

LIMITS
    300,000 cells, 8,192 bytes a cell, files of 32 MB. No charts, no
    sorting, no names for ranges, no whole columns (A:A) in formulas,
    no dates. A recalculation too big for SurfOS stops, and the cells
    it did not finish show #LIMIT!.
