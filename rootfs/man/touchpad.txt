touchpad - native mouse devices and the touchpad

SYNOPSIS
    touchpad [info|up|report|descriptor]
    pointerinfo

DESCRIPTION
    pointerinfo says what pointing devices there are, USB, PS/2 and I2C,
    and where the pointer is. touchpad says how the laptop's I2C
    touchpad is doing; up starts it again, and touchpad info then shows
    the progress; report and descriptor show its last report and its HID
    descriptor. These commands make no bus transfers of their own.
