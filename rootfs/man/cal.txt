cal - show a calendar

SYNOPSIS
    cal [[month] year]

DESCRIPTION
    Writes a month's calendar, its weeks from Sunday: this month
    without arguments, the whole of a year with one number, and that
    month with two. A lone number is always a year, so cal 26 is the
    year 26.

    The calendar is the Gregorian one for every year, 1 to 9999: the
    days other systems skip in September 1752 are not skipped here.

EXAMPLES
    cal
    cal 11 2026
    cal 2027

SEE ALSO
