lspci - PCI devices

SYNOPSIS
    lspci [-v] [bus:device.function]

DESCRIPTION
    Lists the PCI devices. An address, in hexadecimal as 00:14.3, shows
    that device in full with its 256 bytes of configuration space.

OPTIONS
    -v  The ids with the subsystem, power state, interrupts (MSI, MSI-X)
        and PCI Express link.

EXAMPLES
    lspci
    lspci -v > /usb1p1/LSPCI.TXT
    lspci 00:14.3
