wclock - the desktop's Clock as a program

SYNOPSIS
    wclock

DESCRIPTION
    A window with the time and date, a month's calendar, a stopwatch
    with laps and a timer, chosen in its toolbar or with the keys 1 to
    4. The stopwatch and the timer go on while the window is tucked
    away, and at zero the timer brings it back. Ctrl+C copies what the
    page shows. Esc, Ctrl+W or the close button end it, asking first
    while one runs. The desktop's Apps menu starts it, or brings its
    window back.

    The time is the PC's clock, and SurfOS knows no time zone.
