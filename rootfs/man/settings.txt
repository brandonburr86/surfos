settings - the settings on the stick

SYNOPSIS
    settings
    settings key
    settings key value

DESCRIPTION
    Alone it lists every setting and where they are kept; with a key it
    says that one's value, the values it takes and what it does; with a
    value it changes it, also written settings key = value.

    They are saved in surfos-config/settings.conf on the stick SurfOS
    was started from, one line at a time, so what you wrote there on
    another computer stays. Every start reads them. A change not saved
    on the stick is a failure, and says why.

    A key or a value it does not know is status 2, and a change not
    saved is 1.

KEYS
    telnetd       on or off: the telnet server at startup, on Ethernet
                  or a WPA2 network. No password: your own networks
                  only.
    telnetd.port  The port it listens on, 23 unless set.
    wifi          on or off: join the first saved Wi-Fi network in range
                  at startup, and again when it comes back. On unless
                  set.
    ethernet      dhcp: an address whenever an Ethernet link comes up.
                  off, the default: the dhcp command.
    hostname      Letters, digits and -: the name DHCP requests give,
                  which the router lists. surfos unless set.
    history       on or off: surfsh keeps the lines typed at its prompts
                  in surfos-config/history, as plain text.

EXAMPLES
    settings
    settings telnetd
    settings hostname laptop

SEE ALSO
