lsblk - disks, partitions and ramdisks

SYNOPSIS
    lsblk [-s]

DESCRIPTION
    Lists the block devices: NAME, SIZE, BUS, RM (removable), TYPE and
    MODEL/LABEL, and STATUS, which says why a device may not be changed.
    A disk that was unplugged leaves the list at once, so its name can
    be used again; a mount of it stays, and mount says to umount it.

OPTIONS
    -s  Blocks moved instead: BLOCKS, READ and WRITTEN.

SEE ALSO
    fdisk, mkfs, mount, eject
