wpaint - Paint: pictures drawn, opened and saved

SYNOPSIS
    wpaint [picture]

DESCRIPTION
    A window to draw pictures in: a canvas of up to 4096 pixels each
    way and 16,000,000 in all (4000 by 4000, say), in 32-bit colour.
    It needs a desktop: Apps > Programs and its tile start it, and
    Files' Open with offers it for a picture. Given a picture, it opens
    it. It opens BMP, PNG, GIF and JPEG files (of up to 72 MB) and
    saves BMP (24 bits) and PNG files. A picture's transparent pixels
    are opened onto white, and a PNG of 16 bits a sample is kept at 8;
    Paint says so, and asks before a save replaces such a file.

    The tools, at the left, and in the Tools menu: the pencil, the
    brush, the eraser, lines, rectangles and ellipses (outlined or
    filled), the fill, text in the console's characters at 1 to 8
    times their size, the colour picker, and the selection. The left
    button draws in the first colour and the right in the second; the
    eraser paints the second colour. Under the tools are the chosen
    one's sizes. The colours are under the canvas: a click on the
    palette or a custom colour makes it the first colour (the right
    button, the second), a click on the two swatches swaps them, and
    Edit... takes a colour as #RRGGBB or red, green, blue.

    A line, a rectangle or an ellipse shows while it is dragged, and
    is drawn when the button is let go. Text is typed where it was
    clicked (Enter starts a line) and drawn when a click elsewhere or
    another tool puts it down; Esc drops it. A selection is moved by a
    drag inside it or by the arrows (ten pixels with Shift): its
    pixels float until a click outside, Enter or another tool puts
    them down, and what they left is the second colour. Copy and Cut
    keep them on Paint's own clipboard, and Paste floats them at the
    top left of the view.

    Undo and Redo go back and forth over the last 100 changes, at most
    32 MB of them; a larger change cannot be undone, and says so. The
    canvas's size (Image > Canvas size) keeps its top left, and the
    new part is the second colour. The zoom goes from 12% to 3200%;
    the wheel scrolls, across with Shift, and zooms with Ctrl about the
    pointer; the middle button drags the view.

    Open and Save as are the desktop's choosers, which ask before a
    file is replaced. A file is replaced as edit saves one: a new file
    beside it, renamed over it; Paint never saves over a file that may
    share its space with a .tmp beside it. New, Open and Close ask first
    when there are changes not saved, and again if the picture changes
    before it goes.

KEYS
    P B E L R O      pencil, brush, eraser, line, rectangle, ellipse
    F T K S          fill, text, colour picker, select
    [ ]              a smaller or larger brush
    + -              zoom in, out (Ctrl+0: 100%, Ctrl+F: fit)
    X                swap the first and the second colour
    Arrows           move the selection, or the view
    Enter, Esc       put down what floats; Esc also selects none
    Delete           delete the selection (the second colour)
    Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y   undo, redo
    Ctrl+X, C, V, A  cut, copy, paste, select all
    Ctrl+N, O, S     new, open, save (Ctrl+Shift+S: save as)
    Ctrl+E           the canvas's size
    Ctrl+W           close the window, asking first about changes

LIMITS
    No transparency, no layers, no anti-aliasing. Paint saves no GIF
    or JPEG: Save of such a picture asks for a .png or .bmp name. Its
    clipboard holds pixels for Paint alone; the desktop's clipboard
    pastes text into text being typed, without control characters.
    Paint keeps at most about 126 MB (82 MB at 2000 by 2000); opening
    a picture needs room for the file and the decoder beside that, up
    to about 340 MB for the largest progressive JPEG.
