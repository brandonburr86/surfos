mount - attach and detach volumes

SYNOPSIS
    mount
    mount [-r] device [path [type]]
    umount path|device

DESCRIPTION
    mount alone lists the mounts. With a device it mounts its file
    system at path, /device unless given; the type is found by looking.
    Nothing mounts a USB drive by itself, with one exception: the volume
    SurfOS was started from is read for its settings. The SurfOS boot
    drive mounts read-only, unless it is a stick made bootable by
    copying files onto it.

    umount writes a volume out and lets it go. A device's name stands
    for its mount. Once begun it finishes, even when blocks could not be
    written: those are given up, and said, and the status is 1. To take
    a stick away, use eject.

OPTIONS
    -r  Read-only.

EXAMPLES
    mount
    mount usb1p1
    mount -r usb1p1 /stick
    umount /usb1p1

SEE ALSO
    eject, lsblk, df, sync
