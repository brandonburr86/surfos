fsbench - time a volume

SYNOPSIS
    fsbench directory [MB]

DESCRIPTION
    Writes a file of MB megabytes, 1 to 64 and 4 unless given, in pieces
    of 4 KB, reads it in pieces of 4 KB and of 64 KB, checks every byte,
    and says the rates and how many requests reached the device. The
    file is called fsbench.tmp, in that directory, and is removed
    however it ends. Ctrl+C stops it.

EXAMPLES
    fsbench /hda1 8

SEE ALSO
    readbench, lsblk, df
