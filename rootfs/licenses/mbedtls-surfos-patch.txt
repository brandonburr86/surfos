SurfOS local change to Mbed TLS 3.6.7 (2026-10-12)

The compiled ssl_tls13_keys.c includes mbedtls_ssl_transform_free() before the
raw free in the early-data, handshake and application-transform failure paths.
This releases child cipher contexts after a partial allocation failure.

The original release archive and extracted vendor files remain unchanged.
The reviewed changes and input/output SHA-256 hashes are recorded in source at
third_party/patches/mbedtls-3.6.7-tls13-cleanup.json. tools/patch-webdeps.py creates
the checked build-directory copy used by both the target and host tests.

The original Mbed TLS license is included beside this notice.
