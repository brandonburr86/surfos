# SurfCPU commands

Run from `surfcpu/` (`make <target>`) or from the repository root (`make -C surfcpu <target>`).
On the Mac, toolchain targets re-run themselves inside the `surfcpu-toolchain` container;
board, card and build-host targets run on the Mac. Variables: `BOARD=arty_a7_100t` (default;
the owner's board) or `arty_a7_35t`; `DESIGN=` for the bitstream targets, one of
`arty_a7_baseline` (the milestone 1 design), `surfcpu_arty_a7_bramdiag` (the SoC with the BRAM
diagnostics, milestone 3), `surfcpu_arty_a7_top` (the SoC with SurfBoot, no DDR; the default),
`surfcpu_arty_a7_ddr_bramdiag` (the BRAM diagnostics with the DDR3L, milestone 4) or
`surfcpu_arty_a7_ddr` (SurfBoot with the DDR3L: the design that boots SurfOS); for the A7-100T only,
`surfcpu_arty_a7_gpudiag` (the SoC with the GPU and its diagnostic, no DDR) or `surfcpu_arty_a7_ddr_gpu`
(the DDR design with the GPU; docs/GPU.md section 10). Give `DESIGN=`
every time: without it `build-fpga` and `program-volatile` take `surfcpu_arty_a7_top`, which
stops at DDR_ABSENT. `BIT=file.bit` programs a particular file;
`CABLE_SERIAL=` the serial number of the board's USB cable when more than one FT2232H is
connected; `V=1` shows full commands. A path may hold spaces: quote it as usual
(`BIT="/Users/me/My Releases/top.bit"`); `ARGS` and `DEPLOY_ARGS` are lists of arguments, so a
path with spaces inside them needs quotes of its own
(`DEPLOY_ARGS='--image "/Users/me/My Releases/SURFOS.BIN"'`). No value may hold `"`, `\`, `$` or
a backquote (mk/host.mk).

The states are as of 2026-10-17; the board results are on the owner's Arty A7-100T
([evidence/physical/](evidence/physical/), [OWNER-GUIDE.md](OWNER-GUIDE.md)).

| `make build-fpga FLOW=openxc7 DESIGN=...` | Mac (Docker) | the same with the open-source flow on this Mac (docs/OPEN-FLOW.md), no build-host.conf needed: Yosys, nextpnr and Project X-Ray in the `surfcpu-openxc7` image, every check of the flow, the constraints nextpnr cannot check listed in the output and the manifest; `DRY_RUN=1` prints the commands. Since 2026-10-18 each placer seed of a design's list gets at most 1800 s of placement and routing (`pnr-timeout`), then the next is tried, and a placement is kept only if every clock has the slack the flow's rule wants (max(0.5 ns, 4 % of its period), or the design list's `min-slack`); with every seed thin the best is kept and the build marked THIN (OPEN-FLOW.md section 3) | works: all five designs passed every gate on both boards (BOARD.md section 8), and with the route-through fix the 100T DDR builds at 0bcadbc. On the board: the baseline (m1), the SoC with the diagnostics (m3), SurfBoot without DDR, and both DDR designs built at 0bcadbc (572 checks; SurfOS to its shell) (BOARD.md section 12). A bitstream built before the fix is suspect until rebuilt |
