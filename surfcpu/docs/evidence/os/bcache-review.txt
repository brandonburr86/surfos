SurfOS: a review of the block cache and FAT after audit B1-B4, and its fixes (audit B5-B11)
date: 2026-10-16 (local)
test: tests/host/test_fatfs.c (make unittest): fs/bcache.c, fs/fat.c and fs/fat_mkfs.c as they are, with stand-ins for
      the rest of the kernel, over RAM disks that refuse writes (all, or those touching some blocks) and reads on
      request. "Another task" is a hook that runs inside a device write, while the cache's lock is let go. An oracle
      reads the FAT16 image after every write the disk stores (every point at which a card could be pulled) and checks
      that no entry, and no FAT link on the way, reaches a cluster the first FAT says is free; a census counts lost
      clusters; bcache_audit() checks each disk's account (B9).
before: surfcpu 9b8621c's fs/, include/ and lib/blibc (git archive) built with this test (bcache_audit() stubbed: 9b8621c
        has no account to check)
after:  sc/fs-fix (the commits below)
host:   the Mac (Apple clang, arm64: long is 64 bits), the project's host-test flags, tests/host/pager_shim; and the
        devenv container (gcc -m32) through make unittest (section 3)
