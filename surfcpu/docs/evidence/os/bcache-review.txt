== 7. The review's threaded test (tests/host/test_fatconc.c in make unittest; conc_campaign.sh, conc_campaign2.sh)
  Host threads run the real cache and FAT with a SurfOS mutex that blocks, a wait for a block that blocks until woken,
  irq_save() as one host lock, a watchdog (30 s with no operation finished: exit 3); workers in their own directories
  check B5 after each failed operation and, after a close's write-back that says 0, the file's entry on the disk (B10);
  a reader of another disk makes room, a syncer runs bsync(NULL), chaos makes the disk refuse for milliseconds.
  make unittest: three settings, 3 s each (exit 1 when a check found something).
  The review's two campaigns, unchanged but for `head -2` raised to 4 (the harness now prints its "before the final
  sync" line and its verdict, and the final checks would be cut off):
    d7452f4 (the review's run, 08:45 and 08:49): 44 runs, 8,776,531 operations, 51,372 checked closes: nothing found
    after (11:58-12:08): 44 runs, 1,783,304 operations, 47,810 checked closes: no watchdog, the final syncs 0 and no
              dirty block left, oracle 0, both accounts right, B5 0, the models and the end comparison right, no close
              whose write-back said 0 with the entry not on the disk, no zero-length write, in every run
  The operations differ for two reasons, both of the fixes. In the settings with no syncer and a streaming writer
