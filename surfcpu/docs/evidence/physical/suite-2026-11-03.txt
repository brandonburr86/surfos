6954a75c, the card with sd=rw. The script typed only what is listed and worked on the card only in
/sd0p1/USERS/DOCUMENTS/BT, which it removed.

mount after umount 60 s when it took 60.04 s (the card stays marked as not cleanly unmounted, so the mount counts the
free clusters). Run 2 repeated those two parts with the script corrected: 15 of 15.
