SurfBoot reads SurfOS's files from the owner's card on the board: the integrator's own capture, not a board-test run
The owner installed SurfOS on the card on the Mac (make deploy-sd MOUNT=/Volumes/SURFOS DEPLOY_ARGS="--new-card
--image ../build/card/SURFOS.BIN": the package SurfOS 1.0.0 build fd374065, 388,724 bytes, surfimg verify: valid), put it
back in the Pmod MicroSD and power-cycled the Arty; surfcpu_arty_a7_top (d174d0f) was loaded into configuration RAM.
