The two failing DDR diagnostics placements, rebuilt without BUFGCTRL route-throughs: both pass on the board
(the integrator's own captures). Cause found by ddr-3: nextpnr routed ordinary nets through unused BUFGCTRL I0->O
route-through pips onto the global clock network; the FASM configures no buffer, so round trip and LVS see a wire,
but on the silicon those nets did not arrive (92a2b51 seed 1: seven route-throughs into the EX stage; 69ee491 seed 3:
one into the shifter's bits 20/21). The fix: a chip database without the 32 BUFGCTRL route-through pips, and gclkcheck,
which fails any build that drives a global line from an unconfigured BUFGCTRL. Same netlists, placements, ROMs and seeds
as the failing builds; only the routing database differs.
