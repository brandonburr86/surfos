Board: Arty A7-100T (IDCODE 0x03631093), JP1 and JP2 off; Pmod MicroSD in JA, Pmod VGA in JB+JC (J1 in JB),
Pmod PS2 in JD (its JP1 on VB), a VGA monitor and a PS/2 keyboard attached; the owner's 32 GB card in the slot.
