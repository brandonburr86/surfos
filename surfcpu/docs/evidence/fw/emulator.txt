# fw track evidence: firmware emulator transcripts (2026-10-15, tree at db2e3df plus an uncommitted edit of docs/FIRMWARE.md, hence "dirty tree")

build/fw/host/surfemu runs the real ROM file build/fw/surfboot.mem (or bramdiag.mem) on an
instruction-level model of the SoC (firmware/tests/surfemu.c), with card images made by
mkcard.py behind the SD card model. These are simulated runs of the firmware, not of the
RTL; the RTL system simulation and the board are pending. The UART output, as printed:
