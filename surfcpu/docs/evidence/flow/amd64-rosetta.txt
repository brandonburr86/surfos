Command (probe scripts kept under build/flow/amd64/, not committed):
  docker run --rm --platform linux/amd64 debian:trixie-slim@sha256:a29215f6... sh probe.sh
Result:
  uname -m x86_64, dpkg architecture amd64
  /proc/self/maps of the x86-64 process maps /run/rosetta/rosetta: the binaries are translated
  by Rosetta for Linux, not emulated by QEMU (no binfmt entries are visible in the container)
  apt-get works; OpenJDK 21.0.12 (x86-64) runs and JIT-compiles (Vivado's installer is Java)
  CPUID seen by translated code: "GenuineIntel", SSE4.1, SSE4.2, AES, PCLMULQDQ, POPCNT;
  NOT present: AVX, AVX2, AVX-512, FMA, BMI1, BMI2, F16C, MOVBE, XSAVE
  (the JVM agrees: UseSSE 4, UseAVX 0)
Speed, same probe natively (linux/arm64) and translated (linux/amd64), wall clock:

Conclusion: the container layer of option B works here (Rosetta, not QEMU). Whether Vivado
itself runs (its versions after 2024.1, the missing AVX, long runs, memory) is unproven until
